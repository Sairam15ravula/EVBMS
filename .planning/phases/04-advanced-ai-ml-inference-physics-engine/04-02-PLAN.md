# Plan 04-02: Retrain ML Models, Independent Anomaly Engine & Grounded Gemini XAI

**Phase**: 4 (Advanced AI/ML Inference & Physics Engine)  
**Plan**: 04-02  
**Goal**: Retrain XGBoost and Random Forest models using cell-level dataset splitting to prevent data leakage, evaluate candidates side-by-side, refine independent rule-based safety checks & Isolation Forest anomaly isolation, and build grounded Gemini XAI explainer.

## Requirements Addressed
- **ML-03**: Enhanced State of Health (SoH) and Remaining Useful Life (RUL) forecasting service trained on NASA B0005 & CALCE aging benchmarks.
- **ML-04**: Automated real-time battery anomaly, cell imbalance, and thermal runaway fault classification engine.
- **ML-05**: Explainable AI (XAI) engine generating physical degradation breakdown with fallback resilience.

## Explicit Technical Constraints
1. RUL defined as remaining cycles/time to 80.0% EOL capacity threshold. Prevent data leakage by cell/pack-level dataset splitting.
2. Train both Random Forest and XGBoost for SoH and RUL; auto-select candidate based on validation metrics (RMSE/MAE).
3. Generate sidecar `<model>_metadata.json` with model name, version, algorithm, training timestamp, validation metrics (MAE, RMSE, R²), feature schema version, and SHA256 checksum.
4. Keep rule-based safety engine independent from Isolation Forest score (ML score supplements rules, cannot override safety violations).
5. Treat 55°C as configurable critical thermal warning. Voltage delta (default 50mV) and resistance thresholds configurable per vehicle configuration.
6. Gemini XAI uses configurable `GEMINI_MODEL` env var and must ONLY explain evidence produced by the physics/ML pipeline without fabricating unmeasured physical percentages.
